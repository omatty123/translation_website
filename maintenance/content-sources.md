## Publications

- **Hyech’o’s “Like the Moon through the Clouds”**: Matty Wegehaupt is credited for chapter 7, pp. 138–160, in _Korean Buddhism: Selected Readings from Primary Texts_, ed. Jin Y. Park and Sumi Lee (University of Hawaiʻi Press, 2026). Use “contribution”; front matter does not separately specify translation and introduction roles. Source: https://uhpress.hawaii.edu/title/korean-buddhism-selected-readings-from-primary-texts/
- **Pure and Fragrant**: Beopjeong; translation and introduction by Matty Wegehaupt. Jogye Order of Korean Buddhism, print 2021; ebook 2023. The page shows the first print date, while its existing Google Books destination describes the ebook. Sources: https://www.jokb.org/bbs/board.php?bo_table=7010&wr_id=76 and https://books.google.com/books/about/Pure_and_Fragrant.html?id=qQ_qEAAAQBAJ . Publisher-indexed copyright text explicitly identifies December 31, 2021 hardcover and December 15, 2023 ebook.
- **May All Beings Be Happy**: Beop Jeong; edited by Shiva Ryu; translated by Matty Wegehaupt. The Good Life, 2006 English edition. Source: https://m.yes24.com/Goods/Detail/1997347 . The 2012 contributor biography uses 2005; newer biographies and the specific English edition record give 2006. Preserve each publication’s author-name form.
- **Hyecho’s Memoirs of a Pilgrimage to the Five Indian Kingdoms**: translated by Matty Wegehaupt in _Korean Buddhist Culture: Accounts of a Pilgrimage, Monuments, and Eminent Monks_, ed. Roderick Whitfield, Collected Works of Korean Buddhism vol. 10 (Jogye Order, 2012). Source: https://www.buddhistelibrary.org/en/albums/central/Dhamma_books/10_Culture_web.pdf ; PDF pages 4–5, 28, 33. Preserve Hyecho and Hyech’o according to the respective works.
- **Temple Fraternities**: Sangkil Han, author; Matty Wegehaupt, translator. _Journal of Korean Religions_ 3, no. 1 (April 2012), pp. 29–63, University of Hawaiʻi Press. The retained public PDF says “Translated by Matty Wegehaupt” on printed page 59. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1353/jkr.2012.0012 . The earlier paired names could imply coauthorship; the new layout distinguishes roles.
- **Montafon Moonlight**: Jeong Chanju’s autobiographical novel about his encounters with Austrian composer Herbert Willi. English translation by Matty Wegehaupt; artwork by Jeong Yungyeong. Public sources: https://omatty123.github.io/MontafonMoonlight/ and https://omatty123.github.io/MontafonMoonlight/preface.html . No print publication date or serialization frequency is asserted.
